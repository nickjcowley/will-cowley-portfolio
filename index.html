Metal-cutting CNC mill

2026 to 2027, in progress

My first CNC router could cut wood well but flexed too much for metal, and adhesive workholding failed on metal parts. So I designed a second machine from scratch in Fusion 360, aimed at stiffness.

It uses a fixed steel bridge with a moving table, profile linear rails and ballscrews. The box-section columns will be filled with a sand and oil mix to damp vibration. The bench and machine frame are welded and built; next I fit the ballscrews and install the electronics.

Man-overboard recovery system

A-level Design Technology project, 2025 to 2026, grade A*

Most yacht safety kit only works if the person in the water can help themselves. My client, an experienced skipper with a 31 ft Moody yacht in Cardiff Bay, wanted a way to recover someone who is unconscious or exhausted.

I designed a two-part system. A hook on a pole threads a rope through the casualty's lifejacket loop, so they are secured without having to grab anything. Then a weighted mesh sling is lowered under them and the main halyard and winch roll them up the side of the boat.

I iterated the threading mechanism through three laser-cut prototypes and used Fusion 360 FEA to check the 3 mm aluminium. A prototype later broke exactly where the simulation predicted. I made two versions of the hook: one I sand-cast myself from a 3D-printed pattern, and one CNC-machined with advice from Whitehouse Machine Tools on design for manufacture.

To test it, I jumped into Cardiff Bay in an auto-inflating lifejacket and played an unresponsive casualty. Setup took 2 minutes 30 seconds and I was lifted the 1.2 m to deck level. The system is now kept aboard the yacht.
